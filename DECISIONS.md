# Creator Gig Marketplace — Decision Points

## DP1 · Rejection Strategy
**Decision:** When a creator declines a booking, the client sees the status update to "Declined" on their "My Bookings" page alongside a direct call-to-action button ("Find Other Creators") that routes back to filtered marketplace search results.

**Why:** Providing immediate visibility keeps the client informed and prevents confusion. Offering a direct option to explore alternative creators preserves client engagement on the platform rather than letting them drop out of the funnel after a rejection.

---

## DP2 · Double Booking Policy
**Decision:** Yes, a gig can receive and hold multiple "Pending" booking requests concurrently, and accepting one request does not automatically reject or lock out other pending requests.

**Why:** Service-based creator gigs represent reproducible capacity rather than single physical inventory items. Allowing multiple pending requests prevents a creator's pipeline from stalling while awaiting client responses, leaving final schedule management to the creator.

---

## DP3 · Discovery & Marketplace Ranking
**Decision:** Weighted Hybrid Sorting (Default: Newest First, with user-controlled toggles for Price Low-to-High and Price High-to-Low).

**Why:** A "Newest First" default gives new creators immediate exposure on the platform without requiring existing booking history. Providing instant toggles for price sorting accommodates diverse client budgets while preventing race-to-the-bottom pricing traps.
